🌍 About EcoConnect 360

Technology for a smarter and more sustainable community

💡 The Problem

Villages and communities face problems such as water wastage, overflowing waste bins, unnecessary electricity consumption, agricultural challenges, environmental changes and lack of real-time information.

🚀 Our Solution

EcoConnect 360 uses sensors, ESP32, Wi-Fi and a smart dashboard to collect information, analyze conditions and provide alerts for better decision-making and resource management.
